# EdgeLab product record

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Active traders reviewing live, replayed, and imported executions. They work for long sessions on data-dense desktop layouts, but must also be able to inspect and recover workflows on tablet and mobile.

## Product Purpose

EdgeLab keeps chart context, execution, replay, journaling, review, analytics, and assisted research in one continuous workspace. Success means a trader can move from observing a setup to reviewing its outcome without losing symbol, time range, account scope, or working state.

## Positioning

The product joins a professional multi-chart terminal to a relational trading journal: a trade, note, calendar date, analytics segment, chart viewport, and generated study remain navigable parts of the same evidence trail.

## Operating Context

Users switch between Live, Replay, Journal, Reports, Quant, and Settings; run one to sixteen chart panes; import or synchronize broker activity; and compare performance across accounts. Keyboard use, dense financial numbers, long-running data connections, and recoverable edits are normal rather than exceptional.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the dark trading-terminal character, electric-cyan identity, chart plot area, expert shortcuts, and existing live/replay behavior.
- Treat CME/tick order-flow access and external AI providers as entitled integrations with explicit unavailable states.
- Never represent delayed, stale, partial, synthetic, or unsupported data as live verified data.
- Broker secrets remain server-side; destructive actions require proportional confirmation.
- Public surfaces persuade; authenticated surfaces optimize repeated operation.

## Brand Commitments

EdgeLab is modern, vibrant, fluid, precise, and calm under repeated use. Vibrancy comes from meaningful live color, contrast, depth, and authored motion—not purple gradients, ornamental glass, or generic SaaS cards.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working chart, replay, Journal, settings, authentication, market-data, sharing, indicator, and Quant surfaces. Demonstration values must be labeled synthetic. No customer testimonials or third-party performance claims are approved.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve context across every transition.
2. Make data provenance and health unmistakable.
3. Keep expert actions fast while making recovery obvious.
4. Use one coherent system from landing page to dense terminal.
5. Make financial meaning readable without relying on color alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Core workflows must remain keyboard complete and usable at 200% zoom, with reduced motion, coarse pointers, long and bidirectional content, and textual alternatives for essential chart-only information.
